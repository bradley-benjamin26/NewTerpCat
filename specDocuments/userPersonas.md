# NewTerpCat Users
TerpCat is designed to be used by a variety of users. This document is intended to provide context about the users to inform development.

## Advanced Users
* Researchers including faculty and graduate students
* Need to access resources beyond University of Maryland's holdings
* Often looking for known items
* Familiar with domains and domain's terminaology
* Unfamiliar with library terminology
* May require detailed metatadate to identify the specific item sought

## General users
* Undergraduate students
* Often doing course assignments or entry-level research
* Unfamiliar with research strategies including:
    * controlled vocabularies
    * advanced searching
    * Booleans, wild cards, and quotations
* May struggle to identify a reliable resource
* Unfamiliar with both domain and library terminology
* Search experienced informed by use of social media systems
* May have trouble with information overload
* Seeking materials to meet a requirement for a course/assignment

## Librarians and Staff
* Familiar with library language but not technical language
* May be demonstrating for a class or otherwise teaching the system
* May be helping a student
* May be using the catalog to find a book for interlibrary loan or another service
